### 🚀 Key Highlights & Structure
1. **Introduction & Problem Justification:** Focuses on customer churn in subscription/SaaS businesses, justifying why proactive ML prediction saves 5-7x customer acquisition costs.
2. **Project Objectives & Scope:** 5 SMART objectives and an explicit 5-dimension In-Scope vs. Out-of-Scope boundary matrix.
3. **Methodology & Strategy:** 6-phase extended CRISP-DM lifecycle covering data ingestion, Great Expectations validation, leakage-free temporal splits, RFM & sentiment feature engineering, gradient boosting (XGBoost/LightGBM/CatBoost), and financial cost-utility matrices.
4. **Timeline & Toolchain:** 33-hour sprint timeline (30-35 hours allocated) with milestone criteria and exhaustive Python ecosystem mapping.
5. **System Architecture & Serving Blueprint:** FastAPI REST microservice, Streamlit operations UI, SHAP explainability, and Evidently AI data drift monitoring.
6. **Expected Outcomes & Risk Management:** 6-point risk matrix with concrete mitigations, target ROI ($620k net preserved ARR), and transition plan for Week 2.
